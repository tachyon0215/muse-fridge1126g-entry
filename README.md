# muse-fridge1126g-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9).

## The entry

> My smart fridge films my leftovers, judges my 2 a.m. cheese, and puts ads on its own door — I bought a witness, not an appliance.

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: its three charges are smart-fridge specific (interior camera, snack judgement, ads on the door screen) and land on one punchline — the appliance is a witness against its owner — avoiding the stock milk-ordering joke the brief warns loses.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
